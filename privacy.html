Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Bell's Clipper is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. This page explains what happens when you choose to connect your Google account.

What the app asks Google for

  • Your email address, only to show which account is connected.
  • Access to files the app itself creates in your Google Drive (the "drive.file" permission). The app can see and change only those files, kept in a folder called "Bell's Clipper". It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

What the app does with it

  • When you press "Upload to my Drive", the app uploads a package of your project to that folder and sets it to "anyone with the link", so the people you send the link to can download it.
  • The sign-in token is stored encrypted on your computer only. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else. "Disconnect" in the app removes it.
  • The app does not collect, sell, share or store your Google data on any server, and does not use it for advertising.

Removing access

You can disconnect inside the app, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.
